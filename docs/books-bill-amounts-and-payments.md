# Bill totals and paid bills

The WhatsApp bill flow asks the worker for PAID or UNPAID before SAVE. PAID records a payment the worker says has already happened; it does not transfer money. An unpaid past-due bill can legitimately remain overdue.

The worker must also explicitly confirm the payment method for every new bill, even when the document or AI suggests Cash, Cheque, or another method. The prompt asks the worker to send the actual method. Only that reply confirms it; SAVE cannot accept an unconfirmed suggestion, and later AI edits cannot replace the worker's method. Existing drafts without a recorded confirmation are prompted before saving.

After organization selection, all active customers can be browsed using **More customers** / **Previous customers** or NEXT / PREVIOUS. Each WhatsApp list has at most ten rows. Type `SEARCH: customer name` to search, or ALL to return to the full list. Selecting a customer fetches the full contact from that same organization and sends contact details, addresses, contact people, and custom fields in separate messages. Only lightweight navigation records are cached for large organizations.

Workers can type customer details directly or use `MANUAL: Car Motor LLC; Phone: 0501234567; Email: accounts@example.invalid` even when the Zoho contact lookup is unavailable. Manual entries are stored on the bill without creating a new Zoho contact. The next step asks for the customer's project/work location, then payment status, then the final SAVE confirmation. Project/site appears in the review, saved-bill message, and Zoho bill notes. It is descriptive job information; this flow does not create Zoho Projects records. Payments use the bill date as the recording date.

After selecting the customer, the worker can answer the project and payment fields together, for example `Project: Al Quoz workshop; Payment method: Cash; Payment status: unpaid`. Use the actual payment method and status; a paid bill still requires the actual payment account. The same fields can accompany typed customer details. Labelled customer input still follows the existing explicit selection policy. These combined replies use the deterministic parser, retain the final review and SAVE, and do not change the existing question text or AI model.

For paid bills, workers select the account actually used from an organization-specific WhatsApp list. Accounts are fetched from Zoho and verified again before payment. Optional default account IDs can be configured using the keys in `.env.example`; for example, `ZOHO_BOOKS_CONTRACTING_PAYMENT_CASH_ACCOUNT_ID`. Cash requires an active `cash` account; Credit Card requires `credit_card`; Bank Transfer, Bank Remittance and Cheque require `bank`. Missing defaults open the account picker. If no compatible account exists, the worker must correct the payment method or have the account configured in Zoho. The selected account is displayed in the review and saved-bill report. Changing organization or payment method clears the previous account selection.

Standalone payment-method replies are processed without waiting for the message batching quiet window. Concurrent account-list reads for the same organization share one request sequence; subsequent lookups and final account validation still fetch current Zoho data. These changes leave invoice-page batching and Boss lead processing unchanged.

Books also skips the quiet window for a project/site answer, a valid organization choice, or a currency answer when that worker's unexpired session is waiting for that field. Ordinary invoice text, media pages and editing retain their batching behavior. Replies from the same worker remain serial through delivery.

Bill processing can handle three different workers concurrently by default. `BOOKS_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` accepts 1–3; it does not change the Boss worker. Each bill worker's full reply sequence finishes before their next message is processed. New bill jobs wake the queue after acknowledgement, and ready jobs start as capacity becomes available. The existing invoice-page grouping window, AI models, prompts, and confirmation steps are retained. `books_reply_ready` and `books_job_completed` log `queueWaitMs` separately from processing time so deployment latency can be measured.

Independent currency, tax, and expense-account reads run together during SAVE, as do the fresh bill and payment-account checks before recording payment. Validation failures keep their existing priority, and no financial write proceeds before all required checks succeed. One minute per bill is a performance target, not a timeout that bypasses validation; external service latency and the worker's responses still affect completion time.

For an existing vendor, SAVE overlaps the fresh vendor lookup with payment-account validation, then overlaps duplicate detection with accounting preparation. All started checks settle and keep their original error priority before any bill creation. Newly created vendors retain their original validation order. Parallel reads and writes share a five-request budget per Books client and organization, keeping several workers from multiplying simultaneous API calls; other deployments and external integrations have separate traffic. See [Zoho API limits](https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/introduction/#api-call-limit). End-to-end timing must include initial image queueing, extraction, worker replies and verified saving; individual job timings alone do not prove the one-minute target.

Large customer and vendor lists load in groups of up to three pages after the first two pages. Contacts retain their original order, every required page is validated, and the existing 100-page limit still fails closed. Simultaneous identical lookups share the in-progress traversal within the same organization and contact type; customer searches are also isolated by search text. Each caller receives its own copy. Bill review explicitly reuses a complete vendor list for up to 60 seconds per organization, avoiding a full reload after each worker reply. Failed or incomplete lookups are not cached, and vendor creation invalidates the cache before and after the request. Ordinary lookups remain uncached. SAVE always starts its own fresh vendor recheck inside the existing lock, so cached review data cannot authorize a write against a changed vendor. The initial vendor and customer lists load concurrently while preserving the original prompt order. A synthetic 7,336-customer test covers the full 37-page list in 14 request rounds without changing the customer-selection messages.

For Contracting only, the confirmed invoice name `NEW QAMAR JASI BUILDING MATERIALS TRADING L.L.C (BR)` resolves to the existing `NEW QAMAR JASI BUILDING MATERIALS TRADING LLC` vendor. This explicit alias is in `src/services/books/vendorAliases.js`; it does not strip branch suffixes from other vendors. The canonical vendor must still be present, active, unambiguous and consistent with any supplier TRN supplied. Its canonical name appears in the review. No vendor registration or VAT amount is changed automatically. If the chosen vendor is not VAT registered but the bill includes VAT, the existing review retry message identifies that conflict and the draft records `VENDOR_VAT_TREATMENT_CONFLICT`. Customer/buyer TRNs are never parsed as supplier registration corrections.

The private `AUTHORIZED_BOOKS_PHONES` setting accepts comma-separated worker numbers. Configure both approved bill workers in the deployment environment and restart the backend to load the list. Only those workers enter the bill workflow; Books routing takes priority if a worker is also present in the Boss list. This setting and Zoho credentials stay outside Git.

The OAuth grant needs `ZohoBooks.vendorpayments.CREATE` to record payments, `ZohoBooks.bills.UPDATE` for rounding reconciliation, and `ZohoBooks.accountants.READ` to validate configured accounts, in addition to the existing contacts, bills, and settings permissions. When token metadata confirms missing payment permission, a paid bill is blocked before creation. Refreshing an existing token cannot add missing scopes. See [Zoho vendor payments](https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/vendor-payments/) and [Zoho bills](https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/bills/).

The source's net line amount determines the effective unit rate when it differs from quantity times the printed rate only by rounding. Gross source line amounts retain their net rate to avoid taxing twice. Bill creation requests exclusive, item-level tax calculation. The API's response-only `item_total` is no longer used as an override.

After saving the returned bill ID, the workflow reads that same bill from Zoho and compares its currency and total to the confirmed source. A difference of at most five minor currency units can be reconciled with Zoho's explicit adjustment field, followed by another read. A larger difference, currency mismatch, missing total, paid/partially-paid bill, or unsuccessful reconciliation blocks completion. Source subtotal and tax are preserved in the notes; any rounding adjustment is recorded separately, without inventing a different tax rate. The example AED 700.02 is therefore verified against the actual saved amount before payment or success is reported.

Bill and payment writes have separate durable checkpoints. Retrying attachments or PDFs never creates a second bill or payment. An uncertain payment response stays locked for reconciliation; the API client never automatically repeats a payment POST after a timeout. The saved balance and paid status must both confirm full settlement. A failed read after a confirmed payment ID is an uncertain outcome, not a rejected payment.

Changes are covered by synthetic client and workflow tests. Read-only checks on 2026-09-29 verified the replacement refresh token against both organizations, with all required scopes present and 664 Contracting plus 7,336 Switchgear customers accessible. No live financial write was used for verification. A running deployment must reload its private configuration to use the new token.
