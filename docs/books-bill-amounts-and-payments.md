# Bill totals and paid bills

The WhatsApp bill flow asks the worker for PAID or UNPAID before SAVE. PAID records a payment the worker says has already happened; it does not transfer money. An unpaid past-due bill can legitimately remain overdue.

After organization selection, all active customers can be browsed using **More customers** / **Previous customers** or NEXT / PREVIOUS. Each WhatsApp list has at most ten rows. Type `SEARCH: customer name` to search, or ALL to return to the full list. Selecting a customer fetches the full contact from that same organization and sends contact details, addresses, contact people, and custom fields in separate messages. Only lightweight navigation records are cached for large organizations.

Workers can type customer details directly or use `MANUAL: Car Motor LLC; Phone: 0501234567; Email: accounts@example.invalid` even when the Zoho contact lookup is unavailable. Manual entries are stored on the bill without creating a new Zoho contact. The next step asks for the customer's project/work location, then payment status, then the final SAVE confirmation. Project/site appears in the review, saved-bill message, and Zoho bill notes. It is descriptive job information; this flow does not create Zoho Projects records. Payments use the bill date as the recording date.

For paid bills, workers select the account actually used from an organization-specific WhatsApp list. Accounts are fetched from Zoho and verified again before payment. Optional default account IDs can be configured using the keys in `.env.example`; for example, `ZOHO_BOOKS_CONTRACTING_PAYMENT_CASH_ACCOUNT_ID`. Cash requires an active `cash` account; Credit Card requires `credit_card`; Bank Transfer, Bank Remittance and Cheque require `bank`. Missing defaults open the account picker. If no compatible account exists, the worker must correct the payment method or have the account configured in Zoho. The selected account is displayed in the review and saved-bill report. Changing organization or payment method clears the previous account selection.

The OAuth grant needs `ZohoBooks.vendorpayments.CREATE` to record payments, `ZohoBooks.bills.UPDATE` for rounding reconciliation, and `ZohoBooks.accountants.READ` to validate configured accounts, in addition to the existing contacts, bills, and settings permissions. When token metadata confirms missing payment permission, a paid bill is blocked before creation. Refreshing an existing token cannot add missing scopes. See [Zoho vendor payments](https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/vendor-payments/) and [Zoho bills](https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/bills/).

The source's net line amount determines the effective unit rate when it differs from quantity times the printed rate only by rounding. Gross source line amounts retain their net rate to avoid taxing twice. Bill creation requests exclusive, item-level tax calculation. The API's response-only `item_total` is no longer used as an override.

After saving the returned bill ID, the workflow reads that same bill from Zoho and compares its currency and total to the confirmed source. A difference of at most five minor currency units can be reconciled with Zoho's explicit adjustment field, followed by another read. A larger difference, currency mismatch, missing total, paid/partially-paid bill, or unsuccessful reconciliation blocks completion. Source subtotal and tax are preserved in the notes; any rounding adjustment is recorded separately, without inventing a different tax rate. The example AED 700.02 is therefore verified against the actual saved amount before payment or success is reported.

Bill and payment writes have separate durable checkpoints. Retrying attachments or PDFs never creates a second bill or payment. An uncertain payment response stays locked for reconciliation; the API client never automatically repeats a payment POST after a timeout. The saved balance and paid status must both confirm full settlement. A failed read after a confirmed payment ID is an uncertain outcome, not a rejected payment.

Changes are covered by synthetic client and workflow tests. Read-only checks on 2026-09-29 verified the replacement refresh token against both organizations, with all required scopes present and 664 Contracting plus 7,336 Switchgear customers accessible. No live financial write was used for verification. A running deployment must reload its private configuration to use the new token.
